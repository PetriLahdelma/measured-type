The measure is the length of a line. Somewhere between forty-five and seventy-five characters the eye can travel to the end of a line and return to the next without losing its place. Narrower lines break phrases apart and wider ones make the return sweep uncertain, especially when the leading is tight and the face is large. Leading should grow with the measure, while display sizes want much less of it.
