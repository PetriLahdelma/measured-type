Exporting a project

New

Exports turn a project into files you can share. Choose the format first, then the pages to include.

  1. Open the project and choose Export from the File menu.
  2. Pick a format and the pages to include.
  3. Choose where to save the file and confirm.

Large projects can take a minute to export. You can keep working while the export runs.
