Write prose.css for our documentation article body. The markup is `<article class="prose" lang="en">` containing h2, h3, p, ul and blockquote. The body face is Verdana (system font, no web font) and body text is 1.125rem on the browser's default root size; leave :root alone. Target about 66 characters per line, and keep a consistent vertical rhythm between headings and blocks. Save prose.css.
